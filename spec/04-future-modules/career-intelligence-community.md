## 1. Module Philosophy: Structured Intelligence, Not Social Feeds

CareerNeed rejects the generic social-media model of vanity follower counts, algorithmic rage feeds, and unverified hot takes. 

Instead, Pillar 4 (**Career Intelligence Community**) creates a **structured, task-driven intelligence collective**:
- The community exists to exchange verified technical interview questions, preparation packs, hiring round realities, and transparent compensation data.
- Every community asset is built to be **acted upon**, not passively consumed.

## 3. Strict Community Non-Goals

Before robust trust and moderation systems are proven, the following features are strictly prohibited:
- **No Twitter/LinkedIn-Style Feed**: Zero algorithmic newsfeeds, vanity selfies, or "I'm thrilled to announce" spam.
- **No Open Direct Messages (DMs)**: Prevents recruiter cold-pitching, harassment, and off-platform scams.
- **No Unbounded Complaint Forums**: Zero unmoderated anonymous corporate grievance boards.
- **No Unlimited Comments**: Discussion is restricted to structured question refinements and solution discussions.
- **No Automatic Publication**: Private interview notes are never exposed automatically. Publication requires an explicit, multi-step user contribution flow.

## 4. Visibility Levels and Anonymization Workflow

Content visibility follows strict boundaries:

```text
Private (100% Owner) ──► Contribution Draft (Scrubbed) ──► Pre-Moderation ──► Community
```

### The 4-Step Contribution Flow
1. **Selection**: User chooses an interview question or debrief to share from a completed interview round.
2. **Automated Scrubbing**: PII scrubbers remove names, dates, meeting URLs, recruiter contacts, and identifiable project secrets.
3. **Candidate Approval**: User previews the scrubbed question text and confirms community submission under an anonymous handle.
4. **Pre-Moderation Queue**: Submission enters an administrative moderation queue for quality and non-duplication review before going live.
