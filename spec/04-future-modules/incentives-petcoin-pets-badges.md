## 1. PetCoin: Closed-Loop In-App Recognition Credit

**PetCoin** is an internal, closed-loop community appreciation credit designed to reward high-quality, verified career contributions.

```text
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                       STRICT PETCOIN LEGAL & ECONOMIC BOUNDARIES            │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ • ZERO Cash Value: Cannot be redeemed for fiat currency, gift cards, or cash│
│ • ZERO Crypto / Blockchain: It is an ordinary internal database counter     │
│ • NO Peer-to-Peer Free Transfer: Prevents off-platform trade & money laundry│
│ • NO Speculative or Investment Terminology: Not an asset, token, or security│
│ • NO Job-Search Impact: Zero influence over job matches, ranks, or hiring   │
│ • NOT For Funding AI: Expensive LLM infrastructure cannot be funded by coins│
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### 1.1 Earning PetCoin
Candidates earn PetCoins exclusively through verified, high-value community actions:
- **Approved Question Submissions**: Earning credit after an interview question passes moderation.
- **Accepted Corrections**: Identifying an outdated company interview format or broken link.
- **Community Upvotes / Saves**: When other candidates save a submitted interview pack or study guide to their private pipeline.
- **Delayed Settlement**: Rewards settle after a mandatory 48-hour fraud inspection hold.

### 1.2 Spending PetCoin
Candidates spend PetCoins strictly within the product ecosystem:
- **Unlocking Premium Interview Packs**: Accessing curated, multi-stage prep guides.
- **Fixed Contributor Tips**: Tipping a fixed amount (e.g. 5 PetCoins) to an author whose question directly helped in an interview.
- **Cosmetic Career Pet Accessories**: Customizing their visual companion.

## 2. Career Pets: Gamified Routine Companions

To reduce the emotional toll and isolation of multi-month technical job searches, candidates nurture a **Career Pet**:
- **Default Companion**: Every user has one primary Career Pet.
- **Consistency Flywheel**: The pet evolves and thrives based on consistent career disciplines:
  - Completing daily LeetCode/SQL routines.
  - Submitting applications and tracking follow-ups.
  - Conducting post-interview debriefs.
- **Emotional Support, Not Gatekeeping**: Pet level has **zero effect** on job recommendations, match scores, or AI access. It is purely an encouraging, personal gamification element.

---

## 3. Collectible Badges: Non-Pay-to-Win Recognition

Badges celebrate genuine technical effort and community service:
- **Verified Milestones**:
  - `Code Disciplined`: 30 consecutive days of completed coding routines.
  - `Interview Veteran`: 10 interview rounds debriefed with structured questions.
  - `Knowledge Contributor`: 5 approved interview packs published to the community.
- **Non-Consumable & Non-Purchasable**: Trust and achievement badges **cannot be bought with money or PetCoin**.
- **No Pay-to-Win Advantage**: Badges cannot be used to artificially inflate a candidate's visibility or give unfair advantages.

---

## 4. Explicit Deferral: Company Employee Badges & Verification

Early proposals suggested verifying current employees (e.g. "Verified Google Engineer") to author company packs. This capability is **strictly deferred** beyond Phase 6 due to critical risks:
1. **Privacy & Retaliation Risk**: Publicly identifying current employees exposes them to employer scrutiny or non-disparagement / NDA violations.
2. **Trademark & Legal Exposure**: Using corporate logos and employee titles creates trademark liabilities.
3. **Fraud & Credential Stuffing**: Verifying work emails requires enterprise SSO, domain validation, and active revocation workflows when employees depart.

No company employee verification badges will be developed until enterprise-grade privacy, legal clearances, and automated credential revocation are fully established.
