# Architecture Decision Records (ADRs)

> **Status:** Active Registry  
> **Owner:** CareerNeed Product & Engineering Architecture  
> **Last Updated:** 2026-09-19  
> **Scope:** Index and standards for Architecture Decision Records in CareerNeed.

---

## 1. Purpose of ADRs

Architecture Decision Records (ADRs) capture significant, consequential architectural and strategic product decisions made in CareerNeed. They document the context, considered options, decision rationale, and downstream consequences.

---

## 2. Standard ADR Structure

Every ADR must follow this format:
- **Title**: `ADR-XXXX-<descriptive-slug>.md`
- **Status**: Proposed / Accepted / Superseded / Deprecated
- **Date**: YYYY-MM-DD
- **Context**: The product or technical problem driving the decision.
- **Decision**: The chosen architecture or policy.
- **Consequences**: Positive benefits, trade-offs, risks, and rejected alternatives.
